HOW PRICING WORKS

Know what goes into the estimate.

Indicative launch pricing gives customers a useful starting point. A site visit or scope review establishes the final cost.

Remote photo review Free to request
On-site assessment (eligible jobs receive credit) $49 proposed
Small-job minimum / standard labor $149 / $95 per hour
Purchased materials Supplier cost + 15% handling

Proposed policy: the $49 visit fee is credited in full toward an approved job of $250 or more booked within 30 days; this is not a guaranteed service until operations are approved. Labor rate is for one technician performing eligible work, with a $149 minimum. Travel outside the confirmed core service area, disposal, permits, extra workers, and special materials are separately disclosed in the written estimate. No work or extra fee without customer approval. Regulated work is referred to appropriately licensed providers.
