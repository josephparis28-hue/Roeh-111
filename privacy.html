PRIVACY

Respecting your information matters.

A straightforward outline of how this prototype is designed to handle contact information.

Pre-launch disclosure: This page is a draft and must be reviewed and updated to accurately reflect the final business and every third-party service before collecting actual customer information.

Information provided through the site

Contact inquiries may include your name, email, selected division, project timeline, location (for property inquiries), and details you choose to submit.

How the prototype works

The contact form is currently configured to send your inquiry, including your name, email, chosen service division, and details you provide, to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form-processing provider. Formspree may store and forward the submission to the business account configured by the site owner. The receiving inbox, retention practices, and contact details need to be confirmed before commercial launch. Please do not include sensitive personal, financial, health, or security information.

Website technology

The website uses Google Fonts to display the brand typography. No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts are included in this prototype. Other services must be disclosed here if added.

Before live launch

Identify the final business contact, form processor, retention practices, sharing practices, user-rights request process, and effective date. This draft is not a substitute for a legal review.
