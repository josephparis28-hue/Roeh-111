OUR CUSTOMER PROMISE

Know what you're paying for.

Written price before work

Every project begins with an agreed scope, deliverables, exclusions and any approved third-party expenses.

No forced subscription

Digital customers can own their finished website without a required care contract, and optional support will be clearly priced.

Changes require approval

Additional pages, materials or unexpected work are quoted and approved before they increase the bill.

Proposed policy for Digital: 50% at project kickoff and 50% before publication for the smallest package. Larger projects can use 40% / 40% / 20% milestones when agreed in writing. Work completed to date, cancellations and refunds are handled under the signed proposal, not a blanket promise. Actual final taxes and third-party fees depend on the contract and location.
